Hui Chen

I am a Research Fellow in the School of Computing in National University of Singapore, where I am fortunate to be advised by Bryan Hooi. My research focuses on natural language processing, machine learning and large language models.

Previously, I worked at Nanyang Technological University and received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from Singapore University of Technology and Design. I graduated with a B.Eng. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Zhejiang University.

I am currently working on AI for scientific research and LLM agents. Feel free to chat if you are also interested in these topics!
